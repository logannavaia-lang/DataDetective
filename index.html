What is DataDetective?

DataDetective is a YSWS (You Ship, We Ship) where participants will have access to workshops to learn about cryptography, data analysis, and cybersecurity. They will use what they learn to create projects focused on analyzing, encrypting, and protecting data.

Who is eligible?

This YSWS is for youth aged 13-18. Whether you are experienced with cybersecurity or completely new to the field, DataDetective is designed for all skill levels. Participants do not need previous cybersecurity experience. The workshops will provide beginner-friendly guides and tutorials to help participants learn concepts and turn them into real projects.

What are Classified Cyber Case Files ?

Classified Cyber Case Files are every other day mini events that give you a chance to win exclusive stickers, by using your project to solve problems

what are the shipping requirements?
  • Use real development tools
  • Be unique and distinguishable from existing projects
  • Be functional and reasonably polished
  • Be understandable and usable by someone other than the creator
  • Solve or explore a real problem related to cybersecurity, data, encryption, privacy, or data analysis
  • Be actually shipped rather than remaining an unfinished idea or prototype
what are the costs?

Nothing!! This is funded by Hack Club which funds projects/ysws made by teens for teens making everything 100% free for participants

Cybersecurity Safety

DataDetective focuses on defensive and educational cybersecurity. Projects should focus on protecting, analyzing, encrypting, or improving the security of information and systems. Unauthorized access, credential theft, malware, destructive activity, data theft, or targeting real people or systems without permission are not allowed. Participants should use test environments, their own data, or systems they have explicit permission to test.
